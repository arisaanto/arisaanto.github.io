I am a master’s student in Cognitive Science at the University of Amsterdam. I study cognition where it takes form: in the tools we handle, the spaces we move through, and the relations in between.

Design, to me, is one way the mind realizes itself through its contact with the world. I am interested not only in how technologies work, but in the forms of perception, movement, and relation they make possible. My current focus is physical space as a cognitive instrument, and the technologies that might inhabit it.
