I am a master’s student in Cognitive Science at the University of Amsterdam. I study cognition where it takes form: in the tools we handle, the spaces we move through, and the technologies that respond to us.

Design, to me, is a way of testing what the mind is through its contact with the world. I am interested not only in what technologies do, but in the forms of perception, movement, and relation they make possible. My current focus is physical space as a cognitive instrument, and the technologies that might inhabit it.
