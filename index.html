Hi! I’m Aris, a master’s student in Cognitive Science at the University of Amsterdam.

I’m interested in how we enact cognition through the tools and environments we inhabit. I treat design as a way to advance our understanding of the mind through interaction with the world. Currently, my focus is on physical spaces as cognitive tools and the design of technology embedded within them.
